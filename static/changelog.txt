Version: 2.1.0
Date: 01. 09. 2026
  Features:
    - Renamed mod to 'qol'.
    - Built comprehensive in-game YAFC (Yet Another Factorio Calculator) production planner with React GUI.
    - Added full Factorio 2.0 and Space Age support: quality metrics, discrete fluid temperatures, beacons, fuels, and tech unlocks.
  Refactoring:
    - Optimized RAM prototype catalogs with lazy indexing and immediate temp build memory reclamation.
    - Centralized technology progression tiers and science pack unlocks.
